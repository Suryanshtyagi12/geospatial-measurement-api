Main Learnings & Outcome

Outcome: A robust, secure, and fully dockerized FastAPI backend capable of dynamic spatial transformations, exact geodetic mathematics, and safe zip-slip mitigations.

  • Per-Feature UTM Calculation: Instead of applying a blanket global CRS, this app transforms EPSG:4326 geometries to localized UTM zones on a per-feature basis, ensuring highly accurate measurements globally with minimal distortion.
  • Geodesic Cross-Checks: Integrated mathematically rigorous PyProj Geod cross-checks to constantly compare and validate the planar projected measurements against pure ellipsoid calculations.
  • PyProj Axis Handling: Utilizing always_xy=True during transformations is crucial. Historically, coordinate orders (Lat/Lon vs Lon/Lat) shift between standards, causing widespread spatial inversion bugs if unhandled.
  • Safe Extraction Architecture: Prevented dangerous directory traversal attacks (zip-slip) by explicitly enforcing filename whitelist extractions directly into a sandboxed temp directory instead of trusting internal zip paths.
